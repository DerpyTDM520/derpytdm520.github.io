01 / INTRODUCTION

On the edge of an invented sea

In the fictional Lantern Harbor, every window holds a small light. At dusk the city seems to drift, as if its streets could rearrange themselves before morning.

The scene exists only to illustrate how a long-form reading interface can organize a narrative into distinct, navigable parts.

02 / PERSPECTIVE

A city made of stories

Some residents of the imaginary harbor collect maps. Others collect stories about the places they have never visited. No account is presented as a historical fact.

Spacing, typography, contrast, and reading progression are the actual subjects of this design exercise.
