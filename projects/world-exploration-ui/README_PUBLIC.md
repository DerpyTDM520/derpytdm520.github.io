# World Exploration · Synthetic UI Study

This is a standalone, original, mobile-first interface illustration of a larger unreleased project. All names, locations and story text are invented; the globe and schematic campus are generated locally from custom code. This demonstration is **not** the product APK, an iOS app, a historical dataset or a real campus map.

No external requests, trackers, accounts or personal data are required. Interactions demonstrate navigation, story reading, session-only saves, search, language controls and a schematic campus. The video was captured in Chrome's 390×844 mobile viewport; it is **not** a physical-phone recording.

The actual product is under development. Do not infer editorial approval of any real research or commercial distribution from this demo.
