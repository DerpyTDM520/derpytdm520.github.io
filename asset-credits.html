Casino and shooter development screenshots

Blackjack images are actual Godot 4.5.1 in-engine captures from November 2026, showing the casino room and complete table layout. The prototype reuses the creator's existing game meshes, card designs and table textures. The known Poly Haven lighting HDRI is documented as CC0; this does not by itself establish separate license terms for all inherited models. These screenshots do not distribute editable source assets.

First-person shooter images are captured from a packaged Unreal Engine test run dated October 31, 2026, covering A3 device interaction, A4 defense, A5 enemy activity and the controlled-run completion screen. They are still images, not continuous native gameplay video. Art and commercial-release acceptance are not asserted. Original third-party asset files are not published here.

以上仅为独立研发工程的真实运行截图，不提供原始模型或未经核准的可编辑资源。赌场动作、尸潮美术和完整游戏发行仍在研发中。

Shooter gameplay · sources & attribution

Selected from the existing Windows packaged development build recorded on 15 November 2026. The player was operated by an automatic driver calling the normal movement, firing and interaction handlers. These are actual window recordings—not manually played footage, offline renders or a complete playthrough. Both selected clips are silent, normal-speed, full-frame trims reencoded for web playback.

本次只发布 M4 战斗 20 秒与设施交互 8 秒。含来源记录尚不完整素材的地表近战片段与 60 秒合集未被加入。没有公开任何模型、纹理、引擎工程或音效源文件。

  • M4A1 — Firewarden3D, CC BY 4.0. Adapted scale, part grouping, first/third-person layout and engine materials. Original embedded animations were excluded; the project supplies its own gameplay-driven handling.
  • FPS arms — DJMaesen, CC BY 4.0. Adapted sleeve/glove materials, format and engine integration. Rig templates — Cransh (M4 template) and Cransh (handgun template), CC BY 4.0. Geometry/rig attribution does not imply the original AK or third-party animation package was used. Model sources were obtained through the attributed 3d-fps mirror.
  • Desert Eagle — ELIZION, CC BY 4.0. Scale, origin, rigid parts and engine materials were adapted.
  • Zombie Number 10 — Animated — Tony Flanagan, CC BY 4.0. Adapted units, animation coordinates, root displacement, materials and game-controlled playback.
  • Environment textures and props — Poly Haven, CC0: Concrete Floor (eye-candy.xyz), Corrugated Iron (Dimitrios Savva / Jenelle van Heerden), Concrete Wall 003 (Dimitrios Savva / Rico Cilliers), Barrel 01 (Jorge Camacho) and Industrial Wall Lamp (Kuutti Siitonen). Rendered with project-specific scale, material and lighting adaptations.

Attributions above follow the bundled third-party notices and retained acquisition metadata for the selected scenes. Neither the source creators nor any weapon manufacturer endorses this project. This is not a claim that every asset in the unreleased game has completed a distribution review.
