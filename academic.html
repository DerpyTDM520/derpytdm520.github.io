Undergraduate SRTP · Nonlinear Dynamics

Stability Analysis and Numerical Simulation of a Predator–Prey Model with Habitat Complexity and Diffusion

University of Science and Technology Beijing (USTB) · School of Mathematics and Physics

Investigated predator–prey dynamics in heterogeneous habitats by incorporating a habitat complexity parameter (β) and self/cross-diffusion Laplacian operators into a Holling Type II system. Derived local asymptotic stability conditions via the Hurwitz criterion and simulated phase transitions and 2D spatial Turing patterns in MATLAB.

MSc Dissertation · Social Statistics

Active and Passive Social Media Use and Psychological Distress in the UK: Socio-economic Differences in a Multi-Wave Lagged Panel Analysis of Understanding Society

The University of Manchester · School of Social Sciences

Constructed a pooled person-period panel of 91,283 complete-case observations from 33,027 UK adults across four consecutive lagged transitions (Waves 11–15) of Understanding Society. Separating passive viewing from active posting with baseline GHQ-12 adjustment, the study demonstrates that high-frequency posting retains a modest, consistent positive longitudinal association with subsequent distress while high-frequency viewing does not.

Complete-Case Person-Period N91,283 obs (33,027 adults)
High Viewing vs. High Posting Share77.0% vs. 29.2%

Data source: Understanding Society / UKHLS (UK Data Service, SN: 6614). No microdata downloads.
